Awareness & Prevention

Childhood Nutritional Diseases Guide

Growing bodies need the right balance of calories, protein, vitamins, and minerals. Too little, too much, or missing key nutrients can cause specific health conditions.

Reactions & Eating Behaviors

Food-Related Disorders & Sensitivities

Sometimes health challenges aren't caused by poor diet choices, but by how a child's immune system, tummy, or brain responds to food.

Immune vs. Digestive Response

Food Allergies vs. Intolerances

🚨 Food Allergy (IgE)

The immune system overreacts to foods like peanuts, eggs, milk, tree nuts, or shellfish. Even a tiny crumb can trigger hives, swelling, wheezing, or anaphylaxis.

🥛 Food Intolerance

The digestive system lacks an enzyme (like lactase in Lactose Intolerance), causing bloating, gas, or tummy cramps—uncomfortable, but not life-threatening.

💡 Care Tip: Always check allergy action plans at school and replace avoided dairy with calcium-fortified alternatives.
Autoimmune Gluten Reaction

Celiac Disease

When a child with Celiac disease eats gluten (a protein in wheat, barley, and rye), their immune system damages the tiny finger-like villi lining the small intestine.

Why It Affects Nutrition:

Damaged villi cannot absorb iron, calcium, folate, or calories properly—leading to slow growth, anemia, or tummy pain.

How It's Managed:

A strict, lifelong 100% gluten-free diet (naturally GF rice, corn, fruits, veggies, meats, and certified GF grains) lets the intestine heal completely!

Feeding Behavior & Mineral Clue

Pica (Craving Non-Food Items)

Pica is when a child (older than 2 years) repeatedly craves and eats non-food substances with no nutritional value—such as dirt, clay, chalk, paper, paint chips, or ice.

🔍 Important Nutritional Link:

Pica is frequently triggered by Iron or Zinc deficiency! It also carries risks of lead poisoning, intestinal blockages, or parasites—so pediatric blood tests and safe supervision are essential.

Sensory & Feeding Disorder

ARFID vs. Everyday Picky Eating

🥦 Typical Picky Eating

Common in toddlers! Kids still eat enough variety across food groups to grow steadily and gain weight normally.

🧩 ARFID

Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder involves intense sensory aversion (texture/smell) or fear of choking, causing weight loss, faltering growth, or vitamin deficiencies.

🤝 Supportive Approach: Pressure-free food exploration, pediatric feeding therapy, and targeted supplements help kids expand safe foods gently.
